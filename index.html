Pandughar Hasina Khanom Foundation

Giving back to where we began

Pandughar is the chairman's ancestral village in Muradnagar, Cumilla, and the group carries its name. The Foundation returns the favour: a free school, monthly support for families, safe water and relief when disaster strikes.

500+

Education

Completely free schooling for students in classes 6 to 10 at Pandughar Samsuddin Ahammad Bhuiyan High School, plus scholarships for higher study.

2,540

Monthly support

Rice and a cash allowance every month for elderly and underprivileged families in Pandughar, Gaibandha, Char Rajibpur and Chitalmari.

13

Reports since 2023

Every quarter the Foundation publishes what it did and where, from tube wells in the chars of Kurigram to winter clothing in Bagerhat.
