## Platform

web

## Users

- **Partners and buyers (primary):** global apparel retailers, joint-venture partners, investors and banks judging whether Pandughar Group is a serious, credible counterpart. Their job: understand the group's scale, businesses and leadership quickly, then make contact.
- **Foundation supporters:** people following or supporting the Pandughar Hasina Khanom Foundation. Their job: see what the Foundation does, where, and evidence that it is sustained (quarterly reports).

Job seekers, press and the general public are secondary; Careers and Newsroom serve them.

## Product Purpose

pandughar.com is the corporate home of Pandughar Group, a family-led Bangladeshi conglomerate founded in 1995. It replaces a neglected WordPress site (placeholder text, demo pages, dead links). Success: a partner leaves convinced the group is substantial and well run, and knows how to reach it; a supporter sees the Foundation's work as real and ongoing.

## Positioning

The quiet family group behind brands Bangladesh grew up with: Polar ice cream, Dan Cake and Urban homes. Scale (eleven companies, eight industries, 18,500+ people, export garments for global retailers) told with a human, village-rooted story: the group is named after the chairman's ancestral village, Pandughar in Muradnagar, Cumilla, where most of its charity work happens. The brands are far better known than the parent; the site connects them.

- Stack: Vite + TypeScript, Three.js (turntable), GSAP + ScrollTrigger, Lenis smooth scroll. Deployed to Netlify.
- **Bilingual: Bangla and English** are both required. Type and layout must handle Bangla script.
- Contact form has no backend yet.
- Open: official brand typeface and exact colour codes (palette is sampled from the brand theme image); source for any "exports to N countries" claim; captions for the 14 certificate images.

## Evidence on Hand

- Facts: founded 1995; 11 companies, 8 industries; 18,500+ family members (company-stated); Interstoff buyers include Marks & Spencer, Tesco, H&M, Next; Polar first ISO 22000 dairy company in Bangladesh; Dan Cake is a 50-50 JV with Dan Cake A/S (Denmark); Urban 130+ projects, 2,100+ families housed; IPR licensed by BSEC 2022.
- Foundation: free school (500+ students, classes 6 to 10); 2,540 families get monthly rice and allowance (Jul to Sep 2025); tube wells in Char Rajibpur; 13 quarterly reports May 2023 to June 2026; Nihad's story (Pandughar School to BUET).
- Media: hero video; Foundation photography (`public/media/`); 2026 news photos; 14 uncaptioned certificates.
- Absent, do not fabricate: testimonials, financials, export-country counts, award names.

## Product Principles

1. Brands first, parent second: lead with names people already know, then reveal the family behind them.
2. Proof over adjectives: real numbers, real photos, real reports; nothing invented.
3. Rooted, not corporate: the village of Pandughar is the emotional anchor of the story.
4. Partners can always find the way to talk to us within one move.
5. Bangla is a first-class language, not a translation afterthought.
