Pandughar Group
at a glance

It began with one apartment building in Dhaka. Thirty-one years later, Pandughar builds homes, knits for the world's high streets, makes the ice cream and cakes the country grew up on, and gives back to the village it is named after.

Eight industries,
one family

Real estate came first. Then the lifts and services our buildings needed, export garments, the brands Bangladesh grew up with, and the finance and technology that come next. Drag the disc, or choose an industry.

Giving back to
where we began

The Pandughar Hasina Khanom Foundation works first in Pandughar village, Muradnagar, Cumilla, the chairman's ancestral home and the name the group carries.

  1. 01

    Free schooling

    500+ students in classes 6 to 10 learn for free at Pandughar Samsuddin Ahammad Bhuiyan High School, with scholarships for higher study.

  2. 02

    Monthly support

    2,540 families receive rice and a cash allowance every month in Pandughar, Gaibandha, Char Rajibpur and Chitalmari.

  3. 03

    From school to BUET

    Md. Nihad Ahmed Mollah, a Pandughar School graduate, earned a place at the Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology. Read his story ↗

  4. 04

    Reported every quarter

    13 activity reports since 2023 show what was done and where, from tube wells in Kurigram to winter clothing in Bagerhat. Read the reports ↗
