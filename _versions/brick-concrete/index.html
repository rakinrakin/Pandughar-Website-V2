Back to
where we began

যেখান থেকে শুরু, সেখানেই ফিরে দেওয়া

Pandughar is the chairman's ancestral village in Muradnagar, Cumilla, and the group carries its name. The Hasina Khanom Foundation gives back there first: a free school, monthly support for families, safe water, and relief when disaster strikes.

  1. 500+

    Free schooling

    Classes 6 to 10 at Pandughar Samsuddin Ahammad Bhuiyan High School, with scholarships for higher study.

  2. 2,540

    Monthly support

    Rice and a cash allowance every month in Pandughar, Gaibandha, Char Rajibpur and Chitalmari.

  3. 13

    Reports since 2023

    Every quarter the Foundation publishes what it did and where, from tube wells in Kurigram to winter clothing in Bagerhat. Read the reports
